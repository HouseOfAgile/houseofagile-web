## Most "lost" data is not lost

When a laptop refuses to start, the fault is usually in the power circuit, the screen, or the motherboard - not in the drive that holds your files. When an external drive stops appearing, the file system is often damaged while the data underneath is intact. When an old phone will not charge, the photos are frequently sitting in a backup nobody remembers making.

We have brought back files from a long list of old hard drives, laptops, and phones. The work is patient rather than magical: read the device safely, make a copy, and reconstruct what can be reconstructed from the copy.

## First aid: what to do right now

If a device holding important data has just failed:

1. **Stop using it.** Continued use overwrites deleted files and stresses failing hardware.
2. **Do not format, initialize, or "repair"** the drive when the operating system offers to.
3. **Do not open the drive** and ignore home remedies such as freezing or tapping it.
4. **Write down what happened** - noises, error messages, drops, liquid, the last thing that worked.
5. **Keep everything together** - the device, its charger, cables, and any old computers it was synced with.

## How we work

1. **Diagnosis.** We identify the type of failure and give you a frank assessment of the chances, the effort, and the cost.
2. **Imaging.** Wherever possible we make a sector-by-sector copy of the device first and work only on that copy, so the original is never put at further risk.
3. **Recovery.** File-system repair, partition reconstruction, and file carving on the copy; backup and account extraction for phones.
4. **Verification.** You review what was recovered - folder structure, photos, key documents.
5. **Handover and cleanup.** Data is delivered on a new drive or transferred to your new device. Our working copy is securely erased.

## What we will tell you honestly

- **Encrypted devices need their key.** A locked iPhone without its passcode, or an encrypted drive without its password or recovery key, cannot be read. We do not bypass device locks.
- **SSDs and phones forget quickly.** Deleted files on modern flash storage are often gone for good.
- **Mechanical damage needs a cleanroom.** We recognize it early and refer you on instead of making things worse.
- **Ownership matters.** We work on your own devices, or with documented authorization - for example in the case of an estate.
